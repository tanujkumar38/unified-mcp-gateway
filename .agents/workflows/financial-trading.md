# Financial Trading Mastery Workflow

Activate the financial-trading skill to execute end-to-end institutional market analysis, strategy development, or risk management:
1. Multi-Timeframe Context: Audit active global session (Asian/London/NY) and market regime (ADX/EMA alignment).
2. Advanced Price Action: Map HTF to LTF market structure (HH/HL/LH/LL), Breaks of Structure (BOS), and Market Structure Shifts (MSS) with displacement.
3. Liquidity Engineering: Identify Buy-Side Liquidity (BSL) and Sell-Side Liquidity (SSL) pools, stop clusters, and liquidity sweeps.
4. Supply & Demand / Auction Market: Identify fresh order blocks, Fair Value Gaps (FVG), Point of Control (POC), and Value Area (VAH/VAL).
5. Mathematical Edge: Verify positive Expected Value (EV > 0) and break-even win rate.
6. Position Sizing: Use scripts/position_size_calculator.py for Fixed Fractional (max 1-2%), ATR-based stops, and Half-Kelly fractioning.
7. Risk Discipline: Enforce 1:1.5 minimum R:R, daily loss limit (-2.5%), and 10 cognitive bias de-biasing checks.
